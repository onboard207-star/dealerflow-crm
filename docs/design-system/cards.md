# Cards

## Purpose

Cards group related information when a distinct surface improves comprehension. They provide structure without turning every section into a box.

## Principles

- One coherent purpose per card.
- Surface treatment stays quieter than the content.
- Hierarchy comes from anatomy and spacing before shadow.
- Interactive cards have one clear destination.
- Cards are optional structure, not the default answer.

## Usage Guidelines

Use 16–24 px padding based on density and viewport. A card may contain a header, supporting description, content, footer, and a small number of actions. Use a quiet border and level-0 or level-1 elevation. Dividers separate genuinely distinct regions.

Metric cards must help a decision: provide a clear label, current value, meaningful context, and a trustworthy comparison where relevant. Clickable cards require clear hover and focus treatment and must not conflict with nested actions. Static cards should not lift on hover.

## Do

- Align titles, metadata, and actions consistently.
- Keep related states and controls inside the same surface.
- Use cards to clarify distinct operational concepts.
- Provide loading, empty, partial, and error treatment when data-driven.
- Let cards stack cleanly on narrow screens.

## Don't

- Do not wrap every page section in a card.
- Do not nest cards to manufacture hierarchy.
- Do not use strong shadows or decorative gradients.
- Do not make a card clickable when it contains several competing actions.
- Do not add metrics without a clear decision or action they support.

## Future Considerations

- Standard anatomy for metric, record, insight, and action cards.
- Expandable and draggable cards where research proves value.
- Density variants and dashboard composition guidance.
- AI insight card provenance and review patterns.
